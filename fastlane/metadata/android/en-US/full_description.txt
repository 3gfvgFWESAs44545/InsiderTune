Pixel Notes plays video game soundtracks from <b>khinsider</b> (downloads.khinsider.com).

<ul>
<li>Browse by platform, year, type and charts</li>
<li>Search albums and songs</li>
<li>MP3 or FLAC playback where available</li>
<li>Offline downloads, and Save to phone for real audio files</li>
<li>Optional khinsider sign-in to sync favourites</li>
<li>Last.fm, Libre.fm and ListenBrainz scrobbling</li>
</ul>

Pixel Notes is a fork of ArchiveTune and is licensed under GPL-3.0.
